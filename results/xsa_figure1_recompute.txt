Model

  floor        = rho              / sqrt((1-rho) S2' + rho)

Excess, both readings

CONSISTENCY CHECK against the values the spec states
----------------------------------------------------------------------
  spec: floor 0.200, observed 0.373, excess 0.165, 44%

  THE STATED TRIPLE DOES NOT CLOSE, and the Day-1 gate that
  requires all four numbers can never pass as written.

    observed - floor        = 0.3730 - 0.2000 = 0.1730
    but the spec says excess = 0.1650   (differs by 0.0080)

    0.1730 / 0.3730 = 46.4%, not 44%

  Two of the three stated numbers agree with each other:
    excess 0.165 and 44% are consistent with observed 0.373
    but they require a floor of 0.2080, not 0.200.
    (the percentage alone implies a floor of 0.2089)

  RESOLUTION ADOPTED: report the floor as the recomputed
  0.200 and the excess as 0.173 (46% of the statistic).
  The qualitative claim is unchanged and is what matters: under
  an anisotropy null, well under half of cos(y_i,v_i) is
  self-specific, on XSA's own numbers, at XSA's own scale.

The floor is sensitive to how peaked the attention rows are, so the
single number should always be reported with the assumption that
produced it. That sensitivity is itself an argument for measuring the
null empirically (Check 1, A1) rather than modelling it.
